Privacy Policy — Panen Kilat

Effective October 4, 2026

Panen Kilat (com.panenkilat.game) is an offline game. We do not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

  • On-device data: game progress (money, upgrades, fields, missions, outfits) and simple play statistics (session length, milestones) are stored only in your device's local storage and are never transmitted.
  • Permissions: the game does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.
  • Ads & purchases: this version contains no third-party ads and no in-app purchases. If they are added later, this policy will be updated first.
  • Age: the game is intended for users aged 13 and over and collects no data from anyone.
  • Deleting data: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.
